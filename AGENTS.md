The build logic is in `scripts/` and `.github/workflows/build.yml`. `harfbuzz/`, `build/`, `artifacts/`, `release/`,
`toolchains/` and `.venv/` are generated by the scripts and are not in git; `harfbuzz/` is a HarfBuzz checkout.

- Commit messages: one line, no body, no trailers or attribution.
- `scripts/build.py` builds one target; `scripts/package-apple.py` combines Apple mobile slices; `scripts/make-release.py`
  writes the manifest. Keep the archive layout and `manifest.json` keys stable: the NHarfBuzz binding reads them.
- After a change to `build.py`, build at least linux-x64 (`scripts/build-linux.sh x64`) and check that the smoke
  test ran. `--skip-build` repackages an existing meson build.
